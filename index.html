Real problems,real results

A few examples of how I've helped business owners save time, get paid faster and run a tighter operation.

Inbox & client care

Enquiries were slipping through the cracks

The challenge

Leads came in through several inboxes, web forms and booking partners, with no single view of what needed a reply.

What I did

I built one central booking tracker with status, owner and follow-up date, then set a daily inbox routine to keep it current.

The resultEvery enquiry tracked from first email to confirmed booking
Finance admin

Invoices went out late, or not at all

The challenge

Quotes, deposits and final invoices were handled ad hoc, so payments were missed and the owner had no clear view.

What I did

I set up a quote → deposit → final invoice flow in Xero with owner approval and a daily approvals summary, plus a 14-day payment follow-up rule.

The resultFaster approvals and fewer unpaid invoices
Recruitment

Too many applicants, no time to screen

The challenge

A growing US service business was flooded with applications and the owner was screening them all himself.

What I did

I created a scorecard based on his must-haves and sent each applicant a clear Approve, Flag or Reject call with reasons.

The resultShortlists ready in hours, straight to interview booking
Marketing

Years of customer data, never used

The challenge

Past customer details were spread across form exports full of duplicates, so they couldn't be used for marketing.

What I did

I cleaned and merged the exports into one master list, removed duplicates and set up an email audience ready for campaigns.

The result461 clean contacts ready for email marketing
Systems & SOPs

Everything lived in the owner's head

The challenge

Bookings were handled differently every time, which made delegating and training new people hard.

What I did

I wrote step-by-step SOPs, an email template library and checklists for each type of booking.

The resultConsistent service and easier handover to new team members
Staff support

The team needed more support than the owner had time for

The challenge

Staff onboarding, shift changes, paperwork and everyday questions all landed on the owner, on top of running the business.

What I did

I became the team's first point of contact. I manage shifts and availability in Connecteam, prepare event checklists and staff kits, onboard new team members, and keep every certificate filed and tracked before it expires.

The resultAn organised, supported team, and an owner who's no longer the go-to for every staff question
